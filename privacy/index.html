PartyHop Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

1. Who we are

PartyHop is an app for planning get-togethers with friends and keeping track of your days with and without parties. The app is operated by Timur Rozhkov, an individual based in Georgia ("we", "us"). Timur Rozhkov is the controller of your personal data.

Contact: hiddendesu@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

Account. Your email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your account identifier and the email address that service shares with us (Apple may give us a private relay address).

Profile. Name, username, avatar color, date of birth, gender (you can choose not to say), your vibe (chill or party), and whether you drink alcohol. Your profile photo currently stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Settings. Language, device time zone, currency, theme, privacy and notification settings.

Parties. Title, date and time, place (text or a link), description, participants, the people a party is hidden from, and your response to invitations.

Party reports. What you choose to fill in: money spent, rating, comment. If you turn on alcohol tracking: what and how much you drank and how you felt the morning after.

Friends. Your friends list, friend requests and activity feed.

Technical data. Push notification token and server logs (request time, IP address, error type). We do not collect advertising identifiers, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

Data about alcohol and how you feel is sensitive. Providing it is optional, you can leave it blank, and we process it only with your consent, which you give by entering it and can withdraw by deleting it or turning alcohol tracking off.

3. How we use your data

  • To let you sign in and use the app (performance of our agreement with you).
  • To calculate your streak, points and spending and show your history (performance of the agreement).
  • To make friends, invitations and the activity feed work. Friends see only what your privacy settings allow (performance of the agreement).
  • To send sign-in codes by email and the push notifications you have enabled (performance of the agreement).
  • To check your age: PartyHop is for adults, and alcohol tracking is available only to users aged 18 and over (legal obligation and legitimate interest).
  • To review reports, keep the app secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data, do not show ads and do not share data for advertising.

4. Who can see your data

Other users. Your username, name and avatar color are visible to people who search for you by username. Friends see your parties, participation and stats as your privacy settings allow. Party participants see the party details and each other, except people the party is hidden from.

Service providers that process data on our behalf: - Railway Corp. (USA): server and database hosting; - Resend (USA): sending sign-in emails; - Apple and Google: sign-in with their accounts; - Expo (USA), Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering push notifications.

We may also disclose data when required by law.

5. Where and how long we store data

Your data is stored on servers provided by Railway in the United States. Some of our providers process data outside your country. Where the law requires it, we rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards for these transfers.

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleted parties and deleted accounts are permanently erased after 30 days. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

6. Deleting your account and your rights

You can delete your account in the app, in your Profile. All account data is permanently erased within 30 days. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke our access to your Apple ID.

You can view and correct your data in your profile, limit who sees it in privacy settings and turn off notifications at any time. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing, or object to processing by writing to hiddendesu@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

If you believe we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Service of Georgia or to the data protection authority in your country.

7. Age

PartyHop is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you learn that a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to servers is restricted. There are no passwords: you sign in with an emailed code or with Apple or Google.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and tell you about important changes in the app.
