Where to find help

If you feel that alcohol has become a problem for you or someone close to you, you are not alone. Talking to someone is a good first step. These services are free and confidential.

In an emergency

If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number right now: 112 in Europe and many other countries, 911 in the US and Canada.

Find a Helpline

Free helplines in your country, by phone, text or chat. Pick your country and the topic "Substance use".

Alcoholics Anonymous

Meetings and contacts of AA in your country, in person and online.

Al-Anon Family Groups

Support for people who are worried about someone else's drinking.

Some national helplines

United States

SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357, 24/7. More

United Kingdom

Drinkline: 0300 123 1110. NHS alcohol support
